## Our mission

Our group investigates cybersecurity problems in which intelligent analysis must remain technically grounded, explainable, and reproducible. We work across malware analysis, digital forensics, information hiding, software supply chains, and the security of critical and connected systems.

Our objective is not only to improve detection performance, but also to understand why a system produces a security decision and how that decision can support analysts, investigators, and system designers.

## How we work

We connect methodological research with experimental validation. A typical project starts from a concrete security problem, defines measurable research questions, develops an analysis method or prototype, and evaluates it on reproducible datasets or realistic test environments.

Our thesis topics are designed around the same principle: each student works on a clearly scoped question with an identified supervisor, expected outcomes, and an appropriate experimental or literature-based methodology.
