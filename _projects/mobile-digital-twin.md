This research direction investigates how a rooted mobile Digital Twin can support repeatable malware analysis and forensic acquisition. The workflow combines preventive inspection, runtime telemetry, and evidence reconstruction while keeping analysis stages traceable.

## Research questions

- How closely can a mobile Digital Twin reproduce security-relevant behavior?
- Which artifacts remain stable across repeated acquisitions?
- How can preventive and post-incident evidence be connected?
