This project studies repeatable risk assessment for covert channels and stegomalware. It focuses on channel establishment, operational reach, reversibility, bandwidth, robustness, and post-conditions.

## Research questions

- Which properties make hidden channels operationally dangerous?
- How can analysts compare channels implemented across different carriers?
- Can AI-assisted assessments remain reproducible and auditable?
