## Before applying

Read the topic description and verify that your background is aligned with the expected activities. Contact the listed supervisor with a short introduction, your degree programme, relevant coursework or technical experience, and the period in which you intend to start.

## Thesis proposal

Before the thesis begins, the student and supervisor define the research question, scope, expected contribution, methodology, milestones, and deliverables. For an experimental thesis, the proposal should also identify the datasets, tools, evaluation criteria, and reproducibility requirements. For a literature-based thesis, it should define the review protocol, sources, inclusion criteria, and synthesis method.

## Research work

Students are expected to work independently, document decisions and experiments, and discuss blockers early. Meetings and intermediate milestones are agreed with the supervisor according to the project. Source code, datasets, experimental parameters, and results should be organized so that the work can be reviewed and reproduced.

## Deliverables and responsible conduct

The final material normally includes the thesis manuscript and, where applicable, source code, documentation, datasets or dataset references, and experimental results. Students must follow university rules on research ethics, privacy, security, authorship, and the use of third-party or AI-generated material. Specific requirements are confirmed with the supervisor before submission.
